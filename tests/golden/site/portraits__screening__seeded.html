Good to know

I'm awkward in front of a camera — will this work?

That's most people, and it's my favorite kind of session. Everything is directed: where to look, what to do with your hands, when to move. You'll never be left posing in silence.

What should I wear?

Solid colors and things you already feel good in beat anything bought for the shoot. Bring one backup look and we'll pick together before we start.

Where do we shoot?

In the studio in Asheville or on location anywhere the light is good — your office, a trailhead, downtown at golden hour. We pick the spot to match where the photos will live.

Can you photograph our whole team?

Yes — consistent headshots for a full team in one visit, matched lighting and framing, so the site looks unified even as new people join.

I wear glasses — will they catch the light?

Wear them if you wear them every day; they're part of how people know you. I set the lights and the angle of your frames so the lenses stay clear. If you own a pair with an anti-reflective coating, bring that one too.

Can I bring a second look?

Yes. Tier II and Tier III are built for more than one look. On Tier I, bring a backup anyway — we'll choose the stronger one before we start.

Should I get hair and makeup done?

It's optional. Come the way you'd want to be seen on a good day — hair as you usually wear it, makeup if it's part of your routine, and no new haircut or skincare experiment that week. If you'd like a professional artist there, tell me when we plan and I'll set the timing around them.

How should I arrive?

A few minutes early, with outfits on hangers rather than folded in a bag. For the hour before, skip anything that leaves a mark — tight collars, watch straps — and give yourself a minute to settle in; we start easy.
