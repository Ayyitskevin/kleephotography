Good to know

How fast can you turn a listing around?

Tell me the go-live date and I'll be straight about what's possible — listings move fast and the calendar keeps room for them. If it's urgent, say so on the booking form.

Do you shoot video as well as photos?

Yes — vertical walkthrough reels for social and a horizontal cut for the listing page, shot in the same visit as the photos. Motion is where buyers linger.

How should the property be prepped?

Clean, declutter, lights on, blinds open. I stage lightly as I shoot — a tidy house is most of the battle, and I'll flag anything worth moving before the camera comes out.

What formats do I get?

Web-sized files that upload straight to MLS and portals, social crops for the feed, and full resolution for print — all in one gallery, all yours to download.
